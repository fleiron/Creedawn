The short version

Creedawn does not require an account and does not use advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking. Your profile, learning progress, quiz history, and private reflections stay on your device. RevenueCat processes limited technical and purchase information solely to validate purchases and manage access.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Creedawn ("Creedawn," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Creedawn iOS application (the "App").

The App provides guided Christian learning journeys, quizzes, and private reflection tools. It is designed to work without creating a Creedawn account or sending your name, lesson answers, learning activity, or reflection text to Creedawn servers.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features and remember your progress, the App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • the first name or display name you choose to enter;
  • your selected starting point or faith-learning preference;
  • onboarding status and App preferences, including haptic settings;
  • journey and lesson progress, completion dates, and unlock timing;
  • quiz answers, scores, learned-question status, and review progress;
  • private reflections, including text, reflection type, favorites, and related lesson or scripture context; and
  • a cached access or entitlement status needed to display available features.

This information is stored using Apple's on-device storage technologies, including UserDefaults and SwiftData. Except for the limited purchase and entitlement information described below, we do not receive or remotely access this content through the App.

3. What Creedawn does not use

The App does not include third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, advertising attribution, or cross-app tracking SDKs. We do not sell personal information or use App information for targeted advertising.

We do not collect or transmit the following through the App:

  • email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts, photos, health data, or microphone recordings;
  • advertising identifiers or data used to track you across apps or websites; or
  • the text of your private reflections.

If a future version adds an online account, behavioral analytics, cloud sync, or another data-collecting feature, we will update this policy and any required App Store privacy disclosures before that feature is released.

4. Notifications

If you grant permission, Creedawn can schedule local notifications when a new journey day becomes available. These notifications are created and scheduled on your device. Creedawn does not need to send your progress to a remote notification server.

You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Purchases, Apple, and RevenueCat

The App offers one non-consumable, one-time lifetime purchase under the product identifier dev.com.creedawn.lifetime. It is not an auto-renewing subscription. The transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process information such as your Apple ID, payment details, transaction history, device information, and region under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Creedawn uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain entitlement status, and support purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process:

  • an anonymous App User ID generated for the App;
  • device type, operating system, App version, and similar technical information;
  • Apple receipt and transaction information, purchased product, purchase status, and entitlement status; and
  • timestamps such as when the App or purchase status was last checked.

We use RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier. We do not intentionally send RevenueCat your entered name, email address, reflection text, quiz answers, lesson progress, advertising identifier, or custom attribution data.

Creedawn and RevenueCat do not receive your full payment-card details from Apple. RevenueCat acts as a service provider processing purchase information on our behalf. Learn more in RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6. Service providers and international processing

We share information only where needed to operate the relevant feature:

  • Apple processes App Store distribution, payments, refunds, and purchase records.
  • RevenueCat processes limited technical and purchase information to validate purchases and manage entitlements.

RevenueCat is based in the United States and may store end-user information using infrastructure in the United States. Information may therefore be processed outside your country, where privacy laws may differ. We do not permit RevenueCat to use Creedawn end-user information for advertising.

7. Retention, backups, and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you change it, remove it through available App controls, or delete the App. Deleting the App generally removes its locally stored data from that device.

Depending on your Apple device and backup settings, App data may be included in an iCloud or device backup controlled by Apple. You can manage those backups through your Apple account and device settings.

Because Creedawn does not receive your locally stored reflections or learning history, we cannot retrieve, export, or delete that on-device content for you remotely.

Purchase and entitlement records processed through Apple and RevenueCat may be retained as needed to provide purchase access, prevent fraud, comply with financial or legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You may contact us to request deletion of RevenueCat data associated with the App's anonymous identifier, subject to records we or our providers must retain by law.

8. Children's privacy

Creedawn is a general-audience faith-learning App and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not require an account and does not transmit the locally entered name, learning progress, quiz history, or reflection content to Creedawn.

Parents and guardians should supervise a child's use where appropriate. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us outside the App, please contact us so we can address the request.

9. Security and external links

We use the security protections provided by iOS for the App's local storage. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and the security of local App data also depends on the security of your device, passcode, Apple account, and backups.

The App or these pages may link to services operated by Apple or other third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or when legal requirements evolve. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate in-App or store notice when required.
